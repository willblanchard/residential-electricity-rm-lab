Customer WTP and segmentation

The WTP layer explains why customers respond differently to the same rate signal. It calibrates the customer segments used by the single-price and parallel-options result pages.

Segmentation input

Income and building-level demand curves can become a willingness-to-pay layer: as the effective $/kWh signal rises, discretionary demand scales down before device controls are applied. That calibration informs how customer segments respond to rate signals.
