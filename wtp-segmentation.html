Report WTP formulation

The report converts income and demand-curve inputs into a willingness-to-pay threshold for each building. As the effective price rises, fewer customers remain willing to consume optional electricity, so modeled demand falls toward zero.

WTP = Mean Demand / Beta Mean demand gives the scale of baseline consumption; Beta controls price sensitivity.
Q(p) = Mean x Prob(W >= p) Quantity is the baseline demand multiplied by the share whose WTP exceeds price.
Annual-bill response Because the source model reacts to annual cost, segmentation is weaker than it would be with hourly or seasonal bill attention.
