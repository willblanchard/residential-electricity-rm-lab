Summary / Intro WTP + Segmentation Optimization Model Implementation Single Price Results Parallel Options Results Limitations Takeaway

Use rate design to buy flexibility, then protect against leakage.

The core recommendation is to evaluate rates by realized margin after customer response. A single TOU or demand-charge design can look worse on revenue but better on total economics. Multiple plans can improve targeting, but only if leakage and self-selection do not give away too much of the value.

Recommendation

Decision rule Optimize for margin, not headline revenue. Lost revenue can be acceptable when it buys enough peak reduction and avoided energy cost.
Start with Single price
Stress-test with Parallel options
Watch for Leakage

Managerial implication

Rate menus should be designed like revenue-management products: segment customers, test substitution, measure leakage, and choose the plan mix that improves utility economics after customers respond.

Final line Good electricity pricing is not the rate that collects the most from passive customers; it is the menu that preserves margin when real customers react.
