Optional rates create a second RM problem.

Phase 1 asks what happens if the same portfolio is placed under a flat, TOU, or demand-charge tariff. Phase 2 asks who leaves the fixed-rate pool when TOU or demand-charge plans are offered in parallel. The adverse-selection risk is simple: the customers with the most bill savings are also the first customers to switch.
